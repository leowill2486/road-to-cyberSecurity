In networking, a router acts like a hotel entrance where the Public IP is the unique street address 

and Private IPs are the internal room numbers. 

Port Forwarding is the receptionist directing outside visitors to specific rooms based on their 'service window' (Port).

To keep the hotel safe, a Firewall serves as border security. 

A Stateless firewall is like a guard who only checks IDs for each individual packet, 

while a Stateful firewall is a smarter guard who monitors the behavior of the entire connection to decide whether to permit or deny access.
