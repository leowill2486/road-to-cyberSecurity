AI can process the data, but it cannot replace the "Human Alpha."

AI Strengths: Rapid "stop-loss" through high-frequency, massive-scale processing of known patterns.

Human Strengths: Identifying malicious intent, handling novel variables (Black Swans), and performing complex risk-reward trade-offs.

Key Insight: AI is the quantitative tool; the human is the "Fund Manager" making the final executive decisions.
