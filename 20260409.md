Today, I explored the complex journey of a web request, 

from the initial DNS resolution—involving Recursive and Authoritative servers—to passing through a Web Application Firewall (WAF). 

I learned that every step in this process is a potential battlefield : hackers can use DNS poisoning, IP blocking, or SNI filtering to intercept traffic.

I also realized that security is a matter of trust, 

whether it's the risk of malicious code injection in open-source projects, 

or the multi-layered "fencing" used to restrict some webs. 

Ultimately, I've begun to adopt a Zero Trust mindset,
recognizing that in the world of cybersecurity, the boundary between data and dangerous instructions is often just a matter of how you "package" the flow.
